Clear information is part of the service.

LTMP aims to make service listings understandable and transparent. Trust labels should reflect documented checks, not vague promises or unsupported certifications.

Access labels

Use distinct labels for completely free, free tier, open source, free for eligible nonprofits, temporary trial and paid service. Explain quotas, payment-card requirements and eligibility conditions.

Provider checks

Link to official provider pages, identify the organization behind each tool and review its published terms and privacy information.

Review dates

Show when a listing was last checked and schedule rechecks. If details cannot be confirmed, mark the listing as unverified or pending review.

Report a problem

Users should be able to flag broken links, misleading pricing, impersonation, security concerns or outdated information.

No guarantee

A review is not a guarantee that a third-party service is safe, available, private or suitable for every user. Users should read the provider's current terms.
