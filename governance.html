Transparent rules for a trustworthy directory.

LTMP should publish clear criteria for inclusion, corrections, sponsorship and removal. This page is a policy starter and should be reviewed against the actual operating model before publication.

Listing criteria

Prioritize usefulness, clear ownership, working official links, transparent pricing and understandable privacy terms.

Independence

Clearly disclose sponsorships, affiliate relationships or other conflicts of interest. Payment must not silently change a tool's trust status.

Corrections and removals

Provide a way to report errors and document how listings are reviewed, corrected, suspended or removed.

Credits and ownership

LTMP is a platform initiative of Luminary Technicals. Third-party names, logos and services remain the property of their respective owners.
