👋 Welcome to LTMP

Luminary Telegram Management Panel manages your own Telegram account locally. Nothing leaves your PC.


Step 1 — Edit config.js with your API ID and API Hash from my.telegram.org

Step 2 — Run start.bat to launch the backend

Step 3 — Go to Login / Session in the sidebar to connect your account
