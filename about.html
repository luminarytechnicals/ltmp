LTMP · LUMINARY TECHNICALS

A modular discovery platform from Luminary Technicals

LTMP is designed to make useful digital services easier to discover, understand and access. The platform combines tools developed within the Luminary Technicals ecosystem with a curated directory of independent third-party services.

Our purpose

Help individuals, organizations, charities and community initiatives find practical digital resources without unnecessary cost or confusion.

How the modular model works

The main website acts as the central discovery hub. Focused modules may later live on dedicated subdomains, while third-party services continue to be operated by their original providers.

What LTMP is — and isn't

LTMP is a discovery and information platform. A listing does not mean LTMP owns, hosts, endorses, guarantees or is formally partnered with the listed service.

A Luminary Technicals initiative

LTMP is part of the broader Luminary Technicals ecosystem. Platform credits should be visible, while each third-party provider retains credit for its own products and intellectual property.
